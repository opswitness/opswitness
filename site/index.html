HONEST ALPHA BOUNDARIES

Useful today, clear about its limits.

Included in Community Alpha

  • A private Workspace and Work area on your Mac
  • Review the plan and AI team before anything runs
  • Approve actions, answer questions, pause, continue, and stop
  • Readable results, run history, and local output files
  • Ready-made starting points, repeatable jobs, and approved lessons

Not promised in this release

  • A stability guarantee, SLA, or unattended critical business operations
  • Hosted SaaS, multi-user accounts, or a mobile app
  • AI teams changing their own rules or approving their own lessons
  • Tax, customs, insurance, legal, or other licensed decisions without a professional
  • Running a complete Work through DeepSeek or Grok

INSTALL ON YOUR MAC

Drag to Applications. Run your first Work.

Community Alpha is distributed as an ad-hoc signed Apple Silicon Mac App through GitHub Prerelease. It is not notarized, so macOS may require right-clicking the App and choosing Open on first launch.

  1. Download the DMG and verify its checksum.
  2. Drag OpsWitness into Applications.
  3. Open it and sign in to Codex with your own account.
  4. Turn a fictional customer inquiry into a reviewed reply draft.

Everything the App needs is included, so you do not have to install or configure developer tools. Start with the built-in customer example; it creates a draft but does not send anything.

One download. No dependency setup.
